Backend Engineer, Payments Platform

About the team
The Payments Platform team builds the services that move money for millions of merchants: payment
orchestration, ledgers, payouts and reconciliation. We run high-throughput, event-driven systems where
correctness and reliability matter more than anything.

What you'll do
- Design, build and operate backend services in Go and TypeScript that process millions of transactions a day
- Build event-driven pipelines on Kafka with exactly-once or idempotent processing guarantees
- Own services end to end on Kubernetes in AWS: design, deploy, monitor, and on-call
- Improve reliability: retries, idempotency, circuit breaking, graceful degradation, observability
- Work with product and finance teams on reconciliation and reporting

What you'll need
- 2+ years building production backend services
- Strong experience with Go, or with TypeScript/Node.js and willingness to learn Go
- Experience with Kafka or a similar message broker
- Experience with PostgreSQL and data modeling
- Experience running services on Kubernetes and AWS
- Understanding of distributed systems: ordering, delivery guarantees, failure handling

Nice to have
- Fintech, lending or payments domain experience
- Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code
- gRPC / Protocol Buffers
- Experience with workflow engines such as Temporal
